## Content pillars

1. **Privacy education** — how selective disclosure works (what observers can/cannot see from the ledger). Screenshots of the Public Ledger showing `id`, `status`, and redacted `••••` amount rows.
2. **Building in public** — commit milestones, CI/CD pipeline screenshots, test results, deployment progress. Link the GitHub Actions badge.
3. **Ecosystem & users** — onboardings from the Preview/Preprod testing phase, community shout-outs, screenshots from the onboarding ledger form.
4. **Midnight platform content** — Lace wallet integration, Preview faucet, proof server, Midnight.js SDK snippets, Compact contract highlights.

## Posting cadence

- **3–5x per week**, short threads over single long posts.
- Weekly format:
  - Mon: privacy explainer (thread with ledger screenshot).
  - Wed: build-log update (commit/CI/test evidence).
  - Fri: ecosystem/user spotlight or demo clip (30s screen recording).

## Voice & tone

- Calm, deliberate, trust-first (mirrors the product).
- Never overclaim: no fabricated metrics — the product stewards real Preview users.
- Plain language over jargon: "the amount stays on your device" not "off-chain custody primitives".
- No emoji except a crescent moon `U+1F319` as a signature sign-off.
